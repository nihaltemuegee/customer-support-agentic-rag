# Account FAQ

## How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up" on the homepage and provide your email address and a password.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to receive a password reset email.

## How do I update my email or shipping address?
Go to Account Settings after logging in to update your contact and shipping information.

## How do I view my order history?
Log in and go to "My Orders" to see a list of all past and current orders.

## Can I delete my account?
Yes, contact support to request account deletion. This will remove your saved
information but does not automatically cancel any orders in progress.

## Is my personal information secure?
We do not store real payment card numbers directly; payments are processed through a
secure third-party provider. This project itself uses only fictional, synthetic data.
