# Refunds FAQ

## When am I eligible for a refund?
You're eligible for a refund if your return is received within 30 days of delivery and the
item is unused and in its original packaging.

## How will I receive my refund?
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

## How long does a refund take to appear?
Once approved, refunds typically appear in your account within 5-7 business days,
depending on your bank or card issuer.

## Can I get a refund without returning the item?
In most cases no, the item must be returned first. Exceptions may apply for orders that
never arrived or arrived damaged.

## What if my order arrived damaged or defective?
Contact support with your order ID and a description of the issue. Damaged or defective
items are eligible for a full refund or replacement without needing to pay return shipping.

## Can I get a partial refund?
Partial refunds may be issued for orders with multiple items where only some items are
being returned, or as a goodwill gesture for minor issues at support's discretion.
