# Warranty FAQ

## What warranty do your products come with?
Most products include a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

## What does the warranty cover?
The warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. It does not
cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.

## How do I file a warranty claim?
Contact support with your order ID, a description of the issue, and photos if applicable.
We'll review the claim and let you know if a repair, replacement, or refund applies.

## Is the warranty transferable?
No, the warranty applies only to the original purchaser and is tied to the original order.

## Does opening or modifying the product void the warranty?
Yes, unauthorized modification or disassembly voids the warranty.

## How long do warranty claims take to process?
Warranty claims are typically reviewed within 5-7 business days of submission.
