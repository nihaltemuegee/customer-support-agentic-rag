# Returns FAQ

## What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in
their original packaging.

## How do I start a return?
Contact support with your order ID and the reason for the return. We'll email you a
prepaid return shipping label.

## Which items are not eligible for return?
Final sale items, gift cards, and personal care items (e.g. earbuds, cosmetics) cannot be
returned once opened.

## How long does a return take to process?
Once we receive your returned item, please allow 3-5 business days for inspection before
a refund or replacement is issued.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Yes. Mention that you'd like an exchange when you contact support, and specify the
replacement item you want.

## Do I have to pay for return shipping?
No, return shipping is free for eligible returns within the 30-day window. We provide a
prepaid shipping label.
