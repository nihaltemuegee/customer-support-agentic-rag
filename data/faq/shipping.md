# Shipping FAQ

## How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

## How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a flat $4.99 shipping fee.
Express shipping costs $14.99 regardless of order size.

## How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can also ask this
assistant for your order status by providing your order ID (e.g. "Where is my order ORD-1001?").

## Do you ship internationally?
Currently we only ship within the country. International shipping is on our roadmap.

## What if my package is delayed?
If your package hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact support with
your order ID so we can investigate with the carrier.

## Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
Address changes are only possible before the order has shipped. Contact support as soon
as possible with your order ID if you need to update the address.
